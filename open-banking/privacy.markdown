*Last updated: 14th November 2026*

This notice covers a private, non-commercial application registered with [Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com/) by the owner of aioue.net. It reads the owner's own bank accounts so he can keep his own financial records. It has one user, the owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

## What it accesses

With the account holder's consent, given through his bank's own login and approval screens, the application reads:

- account details (account name, type, identifiers, currency)
- balances
- transaction history

It is read-only. It cannot make payments or change anything at the bank.

## Whose data

Only the owner's own accounts. Transactions naturally include the names of the people and businesses he paid or was paid by. That information is used only as part of his own records.

## How it is used and stored

- Used for personal budgeting and record keeping.
- Stored on the owner's own computers. Access credentials and keys are kept outside any shared or public location.
- Not sold, not shared, not used for advertising or profiling, and not passed to any third party other than the providers needed to fetch it (Enable Banking and the bank).
- Kept for as long as it is useful for the owner's own records, then deleted.

## Consent and withdrawal

Access depends on consent given at the bank, which expires and must be renewed. It can be withdrawn at any time through the bank or through Enable Banking, after which the application can no longer read the account.

Questions about this notice: open an issue at [github.com/aioue/aioue.github.io](https://github.com/aioue/aioue.github.io/issues).

This site's blog pages are separate from the application and are not covered by this notice.
